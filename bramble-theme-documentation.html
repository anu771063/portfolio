Bramble documentation

Bramble is built for pet brands selling food, treats and supplements — products people buy again and again for a specific animal. This guide covers setup, settings and the pet profile system.

Getting started

  1. Install the theme. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes. Bramble appears in your theme library after purchase.
  2. Preview before publishing. Click Actions → Preview to check your content in the new theme while your current theme stays live.
  3. Add your logo and favicon. In the theme editor, open Theme settings → Favicon for the browser icon, and the Header section for your logo. Any aspect ratio works.
  4. Set up your menus. Bramble uses your menu handled main-menu in the header and footer in the footer. Nested menus render as a mega menu — see Sections.
  5. Choose your colours. Theme settings → Colors. Read the note on contrast in Accessibility before changing these.
  6. Set up pet profiles. This is what makes Bramble different from a general-purpose theme. See Pet profiles.
  7. Publish. Actions → Publish when you're happy.
Before you publish Check your product pages on a phone as well as a desktop. Product images, variant options and the feeding plan all reflow at narrow widths, and it's worth seeing your own catalogue in that layout.

Pet profiles

A shopper sets up a profile for their animal — name, species, life stage, weight and anything to avoid. That profile then shapes what they see across your store.

What the profile changes

WhereWhat happens
Product cardsA fit badge shows whether the product suits that animal
Product pageThe feeding plan shows a daily portion and how long a pack lasts
Nutrition panelIngredients the animal reacts to are flagged
HeaderA pet switcher shows who they're shopping for, and lets them change it

The three fit badges

BadgeShown when
Right for [name]The product matches on every detail you've supplied
Check with [name]'s needsIt matches, but contains an ingredient they react to
Not sized for [name]Wrong species, life stage, or outside the weight range

If you haven't supplied fit details for a product, no badge appears and the card looks like an ordinary product card. Nothing breaks — you can add fit data to your bestsellers first and the rest later.

Where profiles are stored

In the shopper's own browser. No customer account is needed, no app is involved, and the information is never sent to you or anyone else. This means a shopper who switches device or clears their browser data will need to set the profile up again, which is the trade-off for not requiring a sign-up.

Settings

Found under Theme settings → Pet profiles.

SettingWhat it does
Use pet profilesTurns the whole system on or off. Off hides the switcher, badges and feeding plan.
Species offeredComma-separated. Defaults to Dog, Cat. Add others if you sell for them.
Life stages offeredComma-separated. Defaults to Puppy or kitten, Adult, Senior.
Weight unitKilograms or pounds. Portions are calculated correctly either way.
Ask about sensitivitiesLets shoppers flag ingredients to avoid.
These values must match your metafields If you list Dog, Cat as species here, the species metafield on your products must use those same words. A product tagged dogs will never match a profile set to Dog.

Metafields

Fit badges, the feeding plan and the nutrition panel all read metafields you create once and then fill in per product. No app is required.

Creating the definitions

  1. Go to Settings → Custom data → Products in your Shopify admin.
  2. Click Add definition.
  3. Set the namespace and key exactly as shown below — use bramble as the namespace.
  4. Choose the matching type, then save.
  5. Repeat for each row. Then do the same under Custom data → Variants for the variant metafield.

Product metafields

Variant metafield

KeyTypeExampleUsed for
net_weight_gInteger2000How long one order lasts

Formatting the guaranteed analysis

One line per row, with a colon between the label and the value:

Crude protein: 28%
Crude fat: 16%
Crude fibre: 3.5%
Moisture: 10%

How the feeding plan is calculated

Daily portion is the animal's weight multiplied by portion_per_kg. A 12 kg dog with a portion_per_kg of 15 gets 180 g a day. How long an order lasts is the variant's net_weight_g divided by that daily portion — a 2 kg bag at 180 g a day lasts about 11 days.

Only fill in what you know Every one of these is optional. A product with no portion_per_kg simply doesn't show a feeding plan. A product with no species doesn't show a fit badge. Partial data is fine.

Theme settings

Colors

Bramble uses four colour schemes, each with a paired background and text colour, plus button, highlight and line colours. Sections let you pick which scheme they use.

SchemeTypically used for
BaseMost of the store — page background and body text
RaisedCards and panels sitting above the base background
AccentHighlighted sections and badges
DeepThe footer and other dark full-width areas

Decorative rules and control outlines are separate settings on purpose. Decorative rules can be soft. Control outlines — around inputs, buttons and selectable options — need to stay clearly visible. See Accessibility.

Typography

Separate fonts for headings and body text, with independent size scales, optional uppercase headings, and letter spacing. Keep heading size at or near 100% unless you have a reason — larger values can cause awkward line breaks on narrow screens.

Layout and Shapes

Page width, page margin and the spacing between sections. Under Shapes you'll find corner radii for inputs, buttons and images, plus Show offset frame behind media — the rule drawn behind and below images that gives Bramble its look. Turning it off gives a flatter, plainer style.

Cart

Group line items by pet shows a household with several animals as one plan per animal rather than a flat list. Use cart notes adds a note field at checkout.

Social media and Favicon

Paste the full URL for each network you use — empty ones are hidden. The default sharing image is what appears when someone shares a link to your store; 1200 × 628px is recommended.

Sections

Header

Logo, main menu, pet switcher, search and cart. A menu with nested items renders as a multi-column mega menu, up to three levels deep. On mobile it becomes a drawer. The sticky header option keeps it visible as shoppers scroll.

Announcement bar

Up to three rotating messages, each optionally linked. Also holds the country and language selectors when you sell in more than one market.

Image banner

A large image beside a block of text. Add an eyebrow, heading, paragraph and up to two buttons. Use a 3:4 portrait image for the intended look.

Featured collection

A row of products from one collection, with an eyebrow, heading and a link through to the full collection. Products show fit badges if the shopper has a pet profile set.

Rich text

A block of text on its own, usually on an accent background. Good for explaining your feeding guidance or sourcing.

Product

The product page. Built from blocks you can reorder or remove: vendor, title, price, variant picker, feeding plan, quantity, buy buttons, pickup availability, description, nutrition panel and share. App blocks and custom Liquid are both supported here.

Product recommendations

Two sets, both automatic. Related products are chosen by Shopify from shopper behaviour. Complementary products are ones you've paired up in the Search & Discovery app.

Product list (collections)

The collection page, with filters down one side and sorting above the grid. Set up which filters appear under Search & Discovery in your admin.

Footer

Menu columns, a text block, a newsletter signup, social icons, payment icons and a Follow on Shop button. Add as many menu blocks as you need.

Custom Liquid

Available on every page. Paste app snippets or your own code.

Templates

Bramble includes templates for the home page, products, collections, the collections list, cart, search, blog, blog posts, pages, a contact page, the 404 page, the password page and gift cards.

To use the contact form on a page, create the page in your admin, then under Theme template choose page.contact.

Subscriptions and repeat delivery

Bramble supports Shopify's native selling plans. Once you've set up a subscription app that creates selling plans, products with one show a delivery selector on the product page — one-time purchase alongside each repeat option, with any saving shown beside it.

The selected plan carries through to the cart and checkout, and appears on the line item so shoppers can see what they've chosen.

Why this matters for pet food The feeding plan tells a shopper roughly how long a bag lasts. Pairing that with a delivery frequency that matches is the single most useful thing you can do for a repeat-purchase catalogue.

Accessibility

Bramble's default colours were measured against the contrast ratios Shopify requires: 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for larger text and non-text elements like borders and icons. All defaults pass.

If you change colours, check two things:

  • Each background and its paired text colour stay far enough apart to read comfortably
  • The control outline colour stays clearly darker than your background — this is what makes form fields and buttons visible to people with low vision

A free contrast checker such as the WebAIM Contrast Checker will tell you the ratio for any pair.

The theme is navigable by keyboard throughout, every interactive control has a visible focus outline, and tap targets are at least 44px so they're comfortable on a phone.

FAQ

Do my shoppers need an account to use pet profiles?

No. Profiles are saved in their own browser. No sign-up, no app, and nothing is sent to you.

What happens if I don't fill in the metafields?

The theme works as an ordinary pet store theme. Fit badges and feeding plans appear only on products where you've supplied the data, so you can start with your bestsellers.

Can I sell for animals other than cats and dogs?

Yes. Add them under Theme settings → Pet profiles → Species offered, then use the same words in the species metafield on your products.

Can I turn off the pet features entirely?

Yes. Theme settings → Pet profiles → Use pet profiles. The theme then behaves as a straightforward store theme.

Will I lose my content if I switch to Bramble?

No. Products, collections, pages and blog posts live in your Shopify admin, not in the theme. Section layouts and theme settings don't carry across from another theme, so you'll set those up again — preview before publishing.

Does the feeding plan give veterinary advice?

No. It's arithmetic on the portion figure you supply, meant as a buying guide. Make clear in your own copy that shoppers should follow their vet's advice for animals with specific health needs.

How do I update the theme?

Updates appear in your theme library. Updating replaces theme files, so if you've edited the code, duplicate your theme first and reapply changes afterwards.
